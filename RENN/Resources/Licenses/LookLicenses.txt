LOOK COLOUR TABLES (LUTs)

The colour lookup tables used by RENN's twelve Looks are film emulation CLUTs by Pat David
(RawTherapee Film Simulation collection, https://rawpedia.rawtherapee.com/Film_Simulation), as
distributed with G'MIC (https://gmic.eu) and exported to .cube in the "G'MIC Film LUTs Collection"
(https://github.com/YahiaAngelo/Film-Luts). The files are bundled unmodified apart from their names.

Upstream, Pat David's film emulation collection is published under the Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International license (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
The bundled LUT files are shared under the same license.

Film stock and brand names are used by the upstream collections only to describe the emulated
stock. RENN does not use them, and is not affiliated with or endorsed by their owners.
